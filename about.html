About The Coffee Paws

Welcome home. Your cup and your furry friend are waiting.

The Coffee Paws is a locally owned neighborhood cafe built on two simple loves: good coffee and good company, the furry kind included. Stop by for a quick morning espresso, a long afternoon with a book, or a catch-up with friends. There is always a warm seat and a friendly face (or paws) ready for you.

Our story

We started with a simple idea: a neighborhood place where people slow down, and where a dog under the table is just another regular.

Every drink is handcrafted, every pastry is baked fresh each morning, and every guest is treated like a regular from the very first visit.

What we care about

  • Fresh every day

    Pastries are baked each morning and coffee is brewed to order.

  • Pets welcome

    Well-behaved furry guests get water, treats and a spot at your feet.

  • Made for company

    Comfy seats, easy conversation and no rush to leave.
